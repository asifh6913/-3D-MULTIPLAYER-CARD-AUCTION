How it works

Nine tables, two seats each. You sit, the bot joins you after a few seconds and the clock starts at 15 seconds. Your bid is held from your wallet and refunded the moment you are outbid. PASS and whoever is left standing wins at the current price — if nobody bids the card returns to the pool.
